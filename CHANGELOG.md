# Changelog

## Unreleased (after 0.1.0)

World events engine, first pieces. Everything here is behind one new feature switch, `story-director.world-events` (**World events**, off by default, declared once in Story Director's chronicle part). With it off every output is as before, with one exception: a pack may now carry the optional `content.conditions` section (job A) and the world-pack sections (see the install check below) and installs whatever the switch says (before, those made the install fail).

- **A pack's spec secrets in NPC State (NSSPECSEC; owner decision 17 Nov: withhold).** Behind `story-director.world-events`; with it off nothing changes (a spec secret is dropped as before, the prompt and every screen are as before). With it on, a pack person's `{ id, gate, text }` secrets are seeded into their dossier (kept as `packId` + `gate`, `from: 'pack'`). While a gate is closed the secret is absent from the narrator's prompt; when it opens (`stage`, `meter`, anchors incl. `fact:` / `learned:`, `place:`, `quote:`, joined by and/or) the narrator gets `Name (may come out): text` and the reveal watch lists it, and a `~ reveal` line with a quote found in the reply opens it (a swipe, delete, Re-read or rewind takes it back). Opening is what `chronicle.world.learned` records as `learned:<cast id>/<secret id>` and `writes_fact` (matched by the pack id). The Director's brief lists unlearned spec secrets under a never-hint rule. Gates are read from the head state at the prompt (so a gate that opens in a reply is revealable from the next prompt) and from the reply's own state at the `~ reveal` line (anchors and place by `ctx.query`, so a Re-read of an earlier reply reads the world as it was then); `quote:` terms are checked at the reveal; `stage` uses the person's pack stages by NPC State's own ladder test (a stage's `milestone` is its cue, never a need); `place:` is the scene's place by name or id, whole (a district under it is not read). Learnable in lines mode (the shipped mode). A new query `chronicle.world.anchors` gives NPC State the recorded anchors. Not built: a gate that names another person (`of <id>`) is read as the owner; hidden lore still has no source for `learned:lore/...`. Old-shape secrets keep `(hidden)`.
- **World events: the engine reads the spec rulings (WEFIX1).** Behind `story-director.world-events`; with it off nothing here runs (no step, no `start.world`), and with it on only a pack that uses these fields steps differently (before, season anchors never held and major templates were never rolled). The daily step is given the chat's calendar: season anchors hold in their calendar season; `date:` anchors and `on` (a one-day, tempo-fixed window at set-in-motion 100%) are read; a "N% per season" chance on an event that requires a season is spread over that season's days (tempo no longer changes its yearly odds) and "100% per season" fires once per stretch on a seeded day; windows opened by an anchor made later the same day open that day; thresholds in `likelier_if` / `less_if` are applied instead of dropping the event; outcome weights are sums and differences of numbers and `power:` / `score:` refs; templates honour `requires_any`, `likelier_if` and `less_if` and major templates are rolled under the major caps; a protected person is killed only by an authored event naming them, and a skipped death says "(<name> survived)" with a Problems note; `start.world` is applied once on the first step (no news, no event, no cap use) and `content.regions` names appear in `{region}` and in the world note. Not built: `arc`, and `standing:` weights (read as neutral). Details in `docs/v2/PACKS.md`.
- **Open templates (WEOPEN).** Behind `story-director.world-events`; with it off, and for any template with no `open` block, every output is as before (checked against the world/engine tip). A minor template with `open: { menu, writes_facts }`, an optional `fallback` and `per` is filled in by the Director from the pack's menu: new `chronicle.world.open` command (stores the Director's answers and the call count in the chat's ledger in the message before the reply), a Quick-profile batched call (up to 4 slots, one retry with the errors, then the fallback), a pure check of each answer (menu bounds, protected people and places, authored reads unless `open: true`, `writes_facts`), `chronicle.daily.slots` / `pending` (the day record and the slots waiting), asked ahead and never blocking a reply. Swipes, deletes, Re-reads, reloads and rewinds in the session reuse the stored answer. Only minor templates are open; generated arcs are not built. Prompt wording recorded in `NSD-WORDING.md`. Tests: `tests/modules/world/open`.
- **Arc planner: react, never resolve (WEARC).** Behind `story-director.world-events`; with it off the planner's brief, its answer and the plan are as before (the rule is added only with the switch on). The brief opens with the rule: "World events resolve by the world's own rolls. Plan how the party and the world react to an Active world event (rumours, preparations, refugees, a letter, a choice), never how it ends: no beat, clock or agenda may decide it, end it or show its outcome (no coronation while a succession is undecided)." A code guard backs it, on the planner's answer only: a planned beat, clock or agenda whose text names an Active event (its title or id) together with one of that event's outcomes, or names an outcome by an id-like name (with a dash, underscore or digit), is left out, with the note "Refused: this <beat|clock|agenda> decides how the world event \"<title>\" ends; the world resolves it by itself. Plan a reaction instead." on the arc card (the review's list) and, once the plan is accepted, in the accept receipt's summary (kept in the command's payload, so a replay says the same; the host's Problems list takes a command's problem only from a capture, so it is not used). If nothing is left the plan cannot be used. `chronicle.world.brief` gains `guard` (id, title and outcome names of each Active event). Not built: the free-text judgement (a beat that decides an event without naming it or an outcome id) waits for the Director judge.
- **Pack install check up to spec v0.14.10 (WEPACK2).** The check accepts and keeps a condition's `open`, `content.regions`, `start.world`, `@@place`, `date:` and `arc-ended:` anchors, `"on"`, weight expressions, thresholds in `likelier_if` / `less_if`, open templates (`per`, `open` menu with ladder direction, `arc`, `fallback`, `requires`, `requires_any`, `likelier_if`, `less_if`), `set:` / `cleared:` gate terms naming a place, a number `age`, and refuses `place`, `region`, `route`, `realm` as ids. Check only, except open templates (WEOPEN, above): the engine side of the rest is not built. Structural, not behind the switch.
- **World events: the v0.10-v0.14.9 seams (WEV10).** All behind `story-director.world-events`; with it off every output is as before. **Event moves and the living cast** (rulings 8, 37, seam audit E1): the daily step's moves are written into the cast store (`where`, `doing`, a new optional `base` slot `{ at, since, routine, by }`, `since` = now; a journey under way is cleared), a release clears the base it set, an unmet person's death sets `alive` false; the day planner is told a based person's base and routine ("staying at <place> because <event title>" when the event gives none), its moves away from the base are dropped by code and no goal pull starts a journey or meeting for them; a withdrawn meeting does not put back someone moved after it. The story's own bases (`cast.story`, a new system step after the reply): a narrator `where:` on a pack person's dossier, a party leave (a stays record, "waits for You at <place>"), a rejoin (cleared); what was last seen is kept in a new optional `told` slot. **With You** (rulings 33-34): someone in the party (not left) or in the scene is not moved by an event or an evacuation; the Journal says "<name> stays with You — <title>" and a deferred move waits for the first tick after they part (dropped if the event no longer holds it). A death of someone with You is refused unless the event says `even_in_party`; then it is a must-happen line in the world note ("<name> dies here: <summary>"), applied when NPC State records the death or by code after 3 replies (`chronicle.world.cue`, a new system step; Problems note); the daily step now knows who has been met (it used to count everyone as unmet). **from** (rulings 35, 38, 40): a move or death with `from` applies only in that place's group; a move without it only while the person is still at their start; `"anywhere"` moves them wherever they are; a death without it applies anywhere. Named moves apply before the same event's evacuation, which leaves the people they placed (ruling 39). **Learned secrets** (ruling 32): `learned:` anchors and `writes_fact` in the install check and the step; `chronicle.world.learned` (a new system step after the reply's daily step) records NPC State's opened pack secrets in the ledger. **Arc planner** (ruling 9): the query `chronicle.world.brief` and the planner's brief carry each Active event as a world-event thread (now, then "outcome decided by the world"), "Still to come" and the conditions in the party's realm. The chronicle's after-reply hook now also runs for a reply with no story lines, only to count a pending death cue. Not built: the react-not-resolve teaching and judge (owner wording), the open-template cast move, a source for `learned:lore/…`.
- **World events aligned to the author spec v0.14.4 (A, D, E, F; WEALIGN).** All behind `story-director.world-events`; with it off every output is as before (checked byte for byte against the world/engine tip and its base). **Conditions:** a ref is read as the condition's declared scope through the packs' map, the same reading the daily step uses: ids, names and aliases; a realm is a `realms` entry (no longer a region or a pack name) and a region in no realm is in the Wilds; a route by its `name` (default `From-To`; the corridor is no scope of its own). A place ref on a region condition is refused, and a clear acts at the scope the condition is held (a region clear clears it for every place in it). Timers are whole days, at least one ("6 hours" and "0 days" are 1 day). `chronicle.conditions` takes `at` (a place, region, realm or route): the conditions that hold there through the scope hierarchy (the city a district sits in, its region, its realm; the daily step's local-clear masks apply). Clear beats set for the whole day across the world line and the daily step: the conditions cleared today are kept in `chronicle.ledger` (a new optional field, a slot) and nothing sets them there again that day. The `set:`/`cleared:` anchors a world line or the Director makes go to the same ledger, and the next daily step takes them into its anchors, which keep the first occurrence for good (seam audit E24). A `placeStatus` condition is refused for the narrator and the Director where a protected cast member lives (or in a district under it); a minor template that would set one is not placed there; a city's `placeStatus` covers its districts (no templates there; the whole group evacuated to the nearest place outside it). **World line:** a realm condition takes the line's place's realm; a route condition (or any other ref) is written `+name@ref`; the one-line cap counts only lines with `conditions:` (seam audit E38). **News:** what the daily step fires and ends becomes one chronicle entry per occurrence (`by: event:world.<id>`, a template's `event:world.template.<id>`; a fixed id from the event and the day, so a Re-read or a later reply's record never makes a second; `reach`; the place it happened, or the seat of its region or realm; dated on its own day, so news of a long skip has already travelled); the end resolves it and is news of its own (E33, E34). Couriers go at the route class speeds of spec §1 (`routeKinds`; the old single pace only for a route kind with no class), routes blocked or slowed by conditions and doubled at a `placeStatus` end, multiplied together; where no courier route joins two places, reach steps (the Wilds are no realm here). News stops at its reach. A world happening is heard when its courier comes (`tag: 'fact'`); a world line's happening is heard at once, fact at its own place and rumour elsewhere until the courier time. News from a hidden place the party has not reached names its region. The News card shows the place and state as heard here and when and how the news came (E36). The World pulse never makes a rumour of a world happening (E35). **World note:** it takes the [World] lines' place while the switch is on (they overlap, E23); its head (the place status, then the conditions here, narrowest first, each with its `means` and told effects, e.g. "all goods ×1.3, grain ×2") is pinned, then the two newest news items heard here and today's world happening at the scene (new query `world.cueToday`). New model-facing note wording, for the owner's approval: "Status here: …", conditions as "name at ref: means (told effects)". A thread kind `world-event` is reserved for world-event mirrors (E32): valid in a saved chat, no capture kind, outside initiative, offers, lapses and News; nothing writes it yet.
- **World conditions store (job A).** `chronicle.conditions` keeps named states of the world (`grain-dear`, `roads-closed`) as `"<name>@<ref>": { name, scope, ref, since, fadesAt, why, by, rev }`, `scope` being place, route, region or realm. The commands `chronicle.condition.set { name, scope?, ref, fades?, why, by }` and `chronicle.condition.clear { name, ref, why, by }` (never a click) are refused with a note when the name is not in the pack's declared list, the scope is not the declared one, or the ref is not a known place, route, region or realm; at most 64 conditions are active and 8 per ref. `fades` is a duration ("10 days", "1 season" = 90 days, "2 months" = 30 days each, "until cleared"); left out, the pack's own. Before each reply a settle step (`chronicle.conditions.settle`) removes the ones whose end has come, with a receipt. Queries `chronicle.conditions({ ref?, scope?, name? })` (active ones only) and `chronicle.conditionNames()` answer null while the feature is off. A pack may declare them as `content.conditions: [{ name, scope, fades?, means }]` (an optional section of pack format 3, kept only when given; `fades` defaults to until cleared). With the feature off no command or query answers, the chronicle has no `conditions` field and every output is as before. Nothing the model reads changes. The condition calls (job B) and the world line's `conditions:` (job D) read and write it through these queries and commands. Since WEALIGN (above): refs are read as the declared scope through the packs' map (a realm is a `realms` entry, not a region or a pack name), timers are whole days, `at` reads the hierarchy, and a same-day clear is not undone.
- **World events: the condition language and the event fields (job B).** With the switch on, a pack event's `when` may call `condition('name')` (a world condition is active at the event's `where`, anywhere when it has none), `condition('name', 'ref')`, `active('event-id')` (the event happened on this line of the story and no `fired:` or `set:` anchor in its `ends_when.any_of` holds) and `fact('text')` (a Journal record mentions the text, any case); conditions are read only through the `chronicle.conditions` query and facts through `director.journal`, so a swipe, delete, rewind or Re-read changes the answer with the store. The pack event fields of the author spec v0.6 §3 (`kind`, `certainty`, `reach`, `severity`, `anchor_mode`, `where`, `requires`, `window`, `chance`, `likelier_if`, `less_if`, `pre_empted_by`, `outcomes`, `effects`, `ends_when`, `cooldown`, `summary`) are checked in the spec's strict grammar and stored: `chance` as "N% over the window" or "N% per season" (recurring), odds modifiers as `×N` (a percent or bare number is refused), the anchor vocabulary (`set:` `cleared:` `fired:` `ended:` `lapsed:` `died:` `departed:` `fact:`, a season, `start`) in `requires`, window `after` (plus an optional duration), modifiers, `pre_empted_by` and `ends_when.any_of`, effects as `name@ref: duration` or `{ condition, at, for }` (stored as objects), `ends_when` as an object, and outcome weights `power:` or `score:`. Every place, route, region, realm (and the Wilds), power, score, condition, cast member and event named must exist in the pack's `packs.content` sections (a section the pack lacks is not checked); `deadline` is no longer a field. Bad ones make the event invalid, with the reason. Nothing uses the fields yet: the daily trigger is a later job, and an event still needs `id`, `cue` and `when` as today. With the switch off the new calls are unknown names and the fields are not read, so pack events behave exactly as before. A ref is matched in any case, as the store matches it. Spec events now install in `content.worldEvents` (see the pack install check below); a legacy `content.events` entry reads the calls at install whatever the switch says.
- **World line carries conditions (job D).** With the switch on, the narrator's `+ world Name — text | state: | place | factions` line may add `| conditions: +name, -name` (and a `region:` label for region or realm conditions): each name is checked against the pack's declared list and set or lifted through `chronicle.condition.set` / `chronicle.condition.clear` (by `narrator`); what the command would refuse (an unlisted name, a place or region the world does not know, no story date yet, a full cap) is found before the call and is a note on the changes card, and the world line itself stands. A region or realm condition with no `region:` label takes the region of the line's place. A route condition (or any other ref) is written `+name@ref` (WEALIGN). A reply may carry one world line with `conditions:`: a second such line is refused with a note (the reply is marked in the chronicle's scratch field `worldLine`); lines without conditions are not capped (WEALIGN, seam audit E38). The teaching sentence is owner-approved (17 Nov) model-facing text, sent only while the switch is on. With the switch off nothing changes: the plain line runs and `conditions:` is ignored.
- **World events: news arrives over the routes (job E).** With the switch on, news of a chronicle entry reaches each place after a delay worked out from the entry's start (`since`) and its place: the courier's time along the shortest chain of routes (new `atlas.courier` query, `modules/atlas/atlas/courier.js`, still used for places only the atlas knows; between the packs' places WEALIGN goes by the route class speeds above, and **Settings › World: news travels this many km a day by courier**, default 60, is the pace of a route kind with no class), and where no chain of routes joins the two places, tiers: the same place at once, the same region 1 day, the same realm 4 days (a place carries a realm only when the atlas gives one), anywhere else 10. Two new queries, `world.newsAt { entryId, place }` → `{ arrivesAt, via: 'route' | 'tier', km?, days? }` and `world.heardHere { place, now }` → the entries whose news has reached the place (default: the scene's place and the Director's clock), answer null with the switch off. Used by the Journal's news (the World screen's News list); the narrator's [World] lines are replaced by the world note while the switch is on (WEALIGN). Nothing is stored: a swipe, an edited Re-read, a middle delete, a rewind or a reload gives the same answer from the same story and clock. A rumour (the World pulse's) is never held back, and an entry with no place or no start time is heard at once; since WEALIGN a world line's far happening is heard at once as rumour, and every entry carries its reach and tag (above). Not built: the Journal's per-day lines (the day digest) are not filtered. Nothing the model is told changes with the switch off (the model-facing text is unchanged either way).
- **World note (job F).** While the feature is on, code adds a short **[World now]** block in the reply's tail (in place of the [World] lines since WEALIGN), about 160 tokens at most: the conditions active at the scene (its place, then its routes, kept as "From-To", its region and realm), the two newest world entries whose news has reached the scene's place, and the day's authored cue when a pack has one. It reads `chronicle.conditions`, `world.heardHere` and `world.cueToday` only (the first two are the world engine's other jobs; with none of them in the host the note is empty), keeps nothing of its own, and so a swipe, a middle delete, a rewind, a reload and an edited Re-read give the note the state gives. With the feature off no line is added and the tail is the base's, byte for byte. A news item whose tag at that place is still rumour is written with " (rumour)" after its title (e.g. `Border War (rumour) — Raiders cross the river`; the tag is the entry's `tag: 'rumour'`, or a pulse rumour event's `rumour` text); a fact has no tag. The wording is approved by the owner (17 Nov) (`modules/world/chronicle/world-note-parts/build.js`). `world.cueToday` exists since WEALIGN (the world happening that fired today at the scene); the note's lines and order are as the WEALIGN entry above says.
- **World events: the daily step (job C).** With the switch on, after each reply's lines code checks the story clock (Story Director's chronicle part, afterCapture). A reply that moved no clock (no time line) moves it on by one hour (`director.advance`, cause `world-clock`); a time line always beats it. A reply that crossed into a new story day carries `chronicle.world.daily`, which steps every day from the last stepped one to today, one at a time (the first step of a chat starts at today; nothing before it is simulated). Each day: condition timers fade; displacements whose condition cleared lapse; the pack's spec events run in the fixed order (set-in-motion, chain stages, one-offs, recurring; by id), then the minor templates. Events: Dormant / Eligible / Active / Ended / Lapsed / Replaced; `requires` and `requires_any`; `pre_empted_by`; the chain cascade (a stage whose only path Lapsed or was Replaced goes the same way the same day; a `requires_any` stage only once every path is gone); a lapse is recorded at the first day after the window, terminal for one-offs and chain stages; `anchor_mode` first/each. Today's chance: the window or per-season chance as a daily rate, odds modifiers on the daily chance, the set-in-motion hazard 1 − (1 − 1/d)^m (certain on the last day; a set-in-motion event whose requirements fail on its last day lapses with a warning); a chance of 0 is not rolled ("blocked (×0)"). The draw is seeded by the campaign seed (drawn once, kept in the chat), the day and the event id, so a swipe never rolls again. Caps: 2 active majors per realm (the Wilds is one), 4 in the world, 1 new major per realm per 45 days; a set-in-motion event fires over them, takes its slots and restarts the timer; a success blocked by a cap is discarded and the event rolls again the next day. Firing picks an outcome (a number, or `power:`/`score:` standing + 100) and applies the event's effects, then the outcome's: clears, sets (no duration on an event = held while it is Active; on a template or `on_end` = the condition's `fades`; at least 1 day; a re-set keeps the longer time; a clear at a ref holds for the rest of that day), powers and scores (clamped to −100..100, no per-deed limits), facts (anchors `fact:<id>`), and named cast (a move is a `displaced` record `{ to, by, until, routine? }`, a death sets `died`; both leave a `died:`/`departed:` anchor and a line on the step's card (the rumour at the place left is not kept anywhere yet: there is no Journal or news entry); a person with `canDieUnmet: false` is not killed, with a warning; templates never touch cast); a `placeStatus` condition set on a place moves its residents to the nearest suitable place. `on_end` applies when an event Ends, not when it Lapses or is Replaced; `ends_when: "instant"` ends the same day. Recurring events pick a place where their `requires` hold (size-weighted; `@place`, `@region`, `@realm` bind to it) with their cooldown per place. Minor templates roll daily (their own "N% per season", else 70% per season), are placed size-weighted by class (capital/city > town > village > outpost = site; class `none` never, unless `where.kinds` names the kind), at most 2 per region per 7 days, with per-place cooldowns, and never on a place under a `placeStatus` condition unless they need it there (a clearing path such as reconstruction). The step keeps its state in `chronicle.daily` (an optional field, absent until the first step, kept as a one-entry list so each step writes it whole: a later step still replays when the reply of the first one is deleted) and writes `chronicle.conditions` (a change to an entry an earlier reply made is marked so it is passed over if that entry is gone; a hold whose event is not Active in the step's state is released); everything is recorded on the reply that carries the step: a swipe or delete of it takes it back, a Re-read runs it again on the new lines, and later replies keep what they recorded. Its card lists what fired, ended, lapsed, was replaced or faded; a skip of more than 30 days adds "N world days simulated"; its warnings go to Problems on that reply. A condition an Active event holds carries `holds` (and `open` when an "until cleared" setter shares it): the settle step leaves it and `chronicle.conditions` counts it active. `chronicle.condition.set` (world lines, the director) now keeps the longer remaining time and any event holds, as the spec's holders rule says. `time.lastLine` does not count the fallback hour, so the note's "the clock has not moved for a while" reminder still works. A route with no `name` is named "From-To", as the conditions store keeps it. With the switch off nothing of this runs: no step, no extra hour, no `daily` field (outputs byte-identical to world/engine and to its base). Not built here: writing deaths and moves into NPC State (they stay in `chronicle.daily.cast`, and every pack person counts as unmet); a tempo setting (tempo is 1; the step itself scales by any tempo 0.25-4); season anchors; drift and the per-deed limits; major templates (the Director's); the warning when an edit removes a later firing's cause; `@route` binding. Real packs now carry spec events, realms and templates (see the pack install check below); events with a power or score threshold are left out by the step with a warning until the engine reads thresholds.
- **Pack install check for the world pack (spec v0.13).** A pack may carry `content.lore`, `placeKinds`, `routeKinds`, `realms`, `powers`, `scores`, `worldEvents` and `templates`, conditions with `effects` and `placeStatus`, places with `id` and `weight`, named routes (`name`; two routes between the same places must each be named; `hours` on a sea route) and a `companion` / `figure` cast, and it installs whatever the switch says (the check is structural; with the switch off the parts are kept and unused, outputs as before). Each broken rule is a Problem naming the path: unmapped kinds, `@` or `: ` in names, duplicate ids across realms/regions/places, undefined conditions, powers, scores, events, templates and people, refs of the wrong scope, legacy ids in anchors, placeholders where they do not belong, a missing `ends_when`, thresholds outside `requires`/`requires_any`/`ends_when.any_of`, bad prices, a template with cast effects, hidden lore with no gate. A bad cast entry is refused alone (the rest load; references to it from other people are dropped with a Problem). Warnings: a `fact:` nothing writes, a place status with no way to clear it, a power also in `ripples.powers`, an unknown season. The full rules and the list of what is not built (the step skips events with thresholds; NPC State is not seeded from these cast entries) are in `docs/v2/PACKS.md`, "The world pack". The check runs in full only when a pack carries one of the eight new kinds; other packs keep the old rules, so their output is unchanged.
- **Lore digest for the director (job G).** `director.loreDigest()` answers `{ lines: [{ title, keys, line, protected? }], rev }`, one line per lore entry (first sentence cut at 100 characters, at most 4 keys) from the open character's lorebook (read by the packs' lorebook reader; the Story Suite's own tagged entries are skipped) and from the enabled packs' lore entries (`packs.content({ kind: 'lore' })`); a lorebook entry is protected when its title carries `[protected]`. `director.loreFor({ place, factions, people })` answers the full text of the entries whose title or keys match (whole words), at most about 4000 characters. The digest is kept between replies and rebuilt only when the source text changed; nothing is stored in the chat. With the switch off nothing is read, asked or answered. On Ersveil (227 lore entries, about 38k tokens) the digest is about 9k tokens.

Firing picks an outcome (a number, or `power:`/`score:` standing + 100) and applies the event's effects, then the outcome's: clears, sets (no duration on an event = held while it is Active; on a template or `on_end` = the condition's `fades`; at least 1 day; a re-set keeps the longer time; a clear at a ref holds for the rest of that day), powers and scores (clamped to −100..100, no per-deed limits), facts (anchors `fact:<id>`), and named cast (a move is a `displaced` record `{ to, by, until, routine? }`, a death sets `died`; both leave a `died:`/`departed:` anchor and a line on the step's card (the rumour at the place left is not kept anywhere yet: there is no Journal or news entry); a person without `canDieUnmet: true` is not killed while unmet, with a warning; templates never touch cast); a `placeStatus` condition set on a place moves its residents to the nearest suitable place. `on_end` applies when an event Ends, not when it Lapses or is Replaced; `ends_when: "instant"` ends the same day. Recurring events pick a place where their `requires` hold (size-weighted; `@place`, `@region`, `@realm` bind to it) with their cooldown per place. Minor templates roll daily (their own "N% per season", else 70% per season), are placed size-weighted by class (capital/city > town > village > outpost = site; class `none` never, unless `where.kinds` names the kind), at most 2 per region per 7 days, with per-place cooldowns, and never on a place under a `placeStatus` condition unless they need it there (a clearing path such as reconstruction). The step keeps its state in `chronicle.daily` (an optional field, absent until the first step, kept as a one-entry list so each step writes it whole: a later step still replays when the reply of the first one is deleted) and writes `chronicle.conditions` (a change to an entry an earlier reply made is marked so it is passed over if that entry is gone; a hold whose event is not Active in the step's state is released); everything is recorded on the reply that carries the step: a swipe or delete of it takes it back, a Re-read runs it again on the new lines, and later replies keep what they recorded. Its card lists what fired, ended, lapsed, was replaced or faded; a step of more than 14 days (or of more than the longest jump setting when that is lower) adds "N world days simulated"; its warnings go to Problems on that reply. A condition an Active event holds carries `holds` (and `open` when an "until cleared" setter shares it): the settle step leaves it and `chronicle.conditions` counts it active. `chronicle.condition.set` (world lines, the director) now keeps the longer remaining time and any event holds, as the spec's holders rule says. `time.lastLine` does not count the fallback hour, so the note's "the clock has not moved for a while" reminder still works. A route with no `name` is named "From-To", as the conditions store keeps it. With the switch off nothing of this runs: no step, no extra hour, no `daily` field (outputs byte-identical to world/engine and to its base). Not built here: writing deaths and moves into NPC State (they stay in `chronicle.daily.cast`, and every pack person counts as unmet); a tempo setting (tempo is 1; the step itself scales by any tempo 0.25-4); season anchors; drift and the per-deed limits; major templates (the Director's); `@route` binding. Real packs cannot carry spec events, realms or templates yet (the pack check at install, a separate job); the step reads `content.worldEvents` and spec-shaped `content.events` once they install.
- **World events: the daily step, seam-audit fixes (E13-E21) and thresholds (v0.13 ruling 18).** With the switch on: **the campaign seed** is minted once before the first reply (`chronicle.world.seed`, a beforeGenerate step with the claim `world:seed`, stored on the message before that reply as `chronicle.worldSeed`), so a swipe or an edited Re-read of the reply that took the first step keeps the seed and its rolls; the step reads its own recorded seed first, then this one (deleting the seed's message keeps the later steps' seed; a new one is minted but not used by them).
